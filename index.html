The Challenge We Solve

Most Visually Impaired Children Never Get to Learn Independently

Over 90% of visually impaired individuals live in low-income settings. Conventional assistive aids are cluttered with high costs, rigid mechanical parts, and inaccessible distribution. Nexyuga rebuilt tactile learning from the ground up.

Severe Literacy Bottleneck

The 99% Braille Literacy Gap

In developing regions, less than 1% of visually impaired children have access to interactive Braille or tactile books. Without early tactile literacy, children face permanent educational barriers in STEM and higher education.

Independence First

Ending Complete Sighted Reliance

Product 01 Tactile & Audio

Meet Vithara

An affordable, durable tactile book system with embedded audio modules. Enables blind and low-vision children to independently touch, feel, and master Braille, STEM concepts, and foundational literacy without constant supervision.
